Legal basis

We process personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Italian Personal Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003, as amended, in particular by Legislative Decree 101/2018).

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, tracking or analytics tools. Consent under the Italian Garante's cookie guidelines (2021) is therefore not required.

Server log files

When you visit this website, the hosting provider processes technically necessary data, such as your IP address, date and time and the page requested. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the secure operation of the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is not merged with other data and is kept only as long as the operation of the site requires.

Fonts (Google Fonts)

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. In doing so your IP address is transmitted to Google, possibly also to the USA. The transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a consistent and appealing presentation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Contacting us

If you write to us by email, PEC or via the contact form, your email program opens. We process your details (name, email address, message) only to answer your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We keep them only as long as necessary or required by law and do not pass them on to third parties without your consent.

Minors

Under the Italian Data Protection Code, children under 14 may send us data only with the consent of their parents or legal guardians.

Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object. To exercise them, contact the email address or PEC above. No Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed.
